About the sunset kayak tour

We launch from the small beach next to the Belém lighthouse at 17:30 and paddle west along the Tagus as the sun goes down behind the 25 de Abril bridge. The route passes the Monument to the Discoveries and the Belém Tower, with a stop on the water for photos and a short history of the river port. Groups are capped at 10 people with two guides, so everyone gets help with their stroke and the double kayaks stay close together.

Price is €45 per person and includes the kayak, paddle, life vest, dry bag for your phone and a hot drink at the end. Children from 10 years old can join in a double kayak with an adult. No experience is needed: the guides give a 15-minute lesson on the beach before launching. Wear clothes you do not mind getting wet and bring a light jacket, because the wind picks up after sunset.

Saturday departures fill up first in summer and on holiday weekends. Each departure shows the seats left when you choose a date. If the river is too rough, we move you to another evening or refund you in full.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the tour? About two hours on the water, plus the lesson and the hot drink, so plan on two and a half hours in total.

Can I cancel? Yes. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before your tour. Cancellations closer to the tour are not refunded, but you can move to another date once at no cost.

Is there parking? There is paid street parking along Avenida de Brasília and a train stop at Belém, five minutes on foot from the meeting point.

Do you run private tours? Yes, the private boat charter takes up to 6 guests with a skipper, from Doca de Alcântara.
